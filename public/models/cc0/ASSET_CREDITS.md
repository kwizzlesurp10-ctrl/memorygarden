# CC0 3D nature models (MemoryGarden 3D walk view)

These GLBs are used only in the **3D walk** view as ambient scenery. Each file is **CC0 1.0**. Attribution is not required; provenance is recorded here for maintainers.

## Packs

| Pack | Author | Page | SPDX |
|------|--------|------|------|
| Kenney Nature Kit 2.1 | Kenney (www.kenney.nl) | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit | CC0-1.0 |
| Ultimate Nature Pack (June 2019) | Quaternius | https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatenature.html | CC0-1.0 |

**Quaternius caveat:** This project ships models from the **June 2019** Ultimate Nature zip whose bundled `License.txt` is CC0 1.0. Quaternius’s current site-wide [license page](https://quaternius.com/license.html) describes a later non-CC0 QAL for newer packs; that does not retroactively change the 2019 pack license stated on the pack page and in the shipped `License.txt`.

Full legal text and pack pages are in [`licenses/`](./licenses/). Machine-readable metadata: [`shortlist.json`](./shortlist.json).
